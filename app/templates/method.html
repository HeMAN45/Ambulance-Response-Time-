Data and leakage rules

Leakage rules

  • No dispatch, travel or assignment timestamps are used. They are recorded after the call and are part of the answer.
  • Only initial call type and initial severity are used, never the final versions assigned later.
  • All aggregates were computed from the training period only.
  • The split is by time: the oldest 80 percent of incidents train, the newest 20 percent test. A random split would leak the future into the past.

Features

  • Time: hour, day of week, month, weekend flag, hour band.
  • Place: borough, zip zone (MODZCTA), dispatch area.
  • Incident: initial call type, initial severity level.
  • Weather: citywide temperature, precipitation, snowfall and snow flag from Open Meteo, plus a US holiday flag.
